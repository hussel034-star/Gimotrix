The Replacement Ladder
L1

Seller Replaces SILENT · FAST

Buyer files claim inside app. Seller replaces within 12h. Payout unfreezes. Rating preserved. No drama.

L2

Seller Fails — Bond Auto-Pays AUTOMATIC

Replacement pulled from the shared pool. Seller's bond is slashed to cover cost. Payout permanently canceled. Rating hit.

L3

3 unresolved claims / 30 days → listings paused. 5 unresolved / 90 days → bond slashed + demoted. 10+ → account suspended, remaining bond used for buyers.

Anti-Abuse Grid
Fake Claim After Full Use

Buyer uses product fully, then files claim at end of window.

Short warranty on consumables; device log proves usage timeline
Duplicate Claims

Same buyer files multiple claims on one order.

One claim per order, permanently locked
Fake Screenshots

Buyer uploads fabricated evidence from gallery.

In-app capture only; metadata verified server-side
Collusive Fraud

Buyer + friend coordinate fake claims.

Device fingerprint + IP clustering detection
Serial False Claimants

Buyer abuses the system across multiple sellers.

3+ false claims → manual review, account limited
Seller Bond Escape

Seller tries to withdraw bond mid-dispute.

Bond locked while any claim is open
